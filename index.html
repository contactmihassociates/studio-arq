The Manifesto
Architecture is the slow art of making light, climate and craft behave — and only after that, of making them photogenic.
01

Site before style

Every project starts with a site visit, a sun-path study and a long conversation. The form follows that — not the other way around.

02

Detail as discipline

The joint between two materials is a tiny ethical decision. We draw every junction so the contractor never has to guess.

03

Climate-first comfort

Chennai heat, monsoon, glare and breeze — we plan for all four before we plan for aesthetics. Spaces should feel good first.

04

One studio, one signature

Architecture, interior, visualisation and supervision live under the same roof — so the design vision never gets translated through middlemen.

How we work

A four-step design journey.

Every project moves through the same four chapters — but the pace, depth and deliverables flex with each brief. Engagements range from a single room to a full ground-up build.

Listen & Brief

Site visit, lifestyle conversation, brief-setting workshop and a written design brief signed off by the client.

Concept & 3D

Sketch options, massing studies, mood boards and a photoreal 3D walkthrough of the preferred direction.

Detail & Drawings

Working drawings, MEP coordination, material specs and a tender-ready BOQ for the contractor.

Build & Hand-over

Weekly site supervision, on-site mock-ups, snag list and a curated styling pass before move-in.

FAQ

Questions we get on first calls.

What does a typical engagement cost?

Design fees scale with project size and scope. We share a clear, fixed-price proposal after the first call — there are no hidden hourly charges. Smaller interior projects start from a flat fee per room; full architectural commissions are billed as a percentage of the project value.

How long does a project take?

Concept to working drawings usually takes 8 – 16 weeks for a residence. Construction depends on scope and contractor — most of our residential builds land between 12 and 18 months. We share a detailed timeline before contracting.

Do you work outside Chennai?

Yes. Our studio is in Chennai, but we have delivered projects in surrounding districts and across Tamil Nadu. For sites further away, we plan periodic site visits and run weekly virtual reviews.

Can I hire you just for 3D visualisation?

Absolutely. Photoreal renders, walkthroughs and material studies are available as a stand-alone service — useful for builders, developers or homeowners working with another architect who want to see the design before committing.

Do you supervise the construction?

For most architecture commissions, yes. We do weekly site supervision, attend critical pours and finishes, and run a snag-list pass before hand-over. For pure interior projects we coordinate with the joinery and finish vendors directly.
