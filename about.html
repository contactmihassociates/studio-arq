The Studio

A small studio with a long brief.

Studio ARQ is an independent architectural practice founded by Abd Al Azeem in Chennai. We design at every scale — from a single room to a multi-storey commercial — and we keep architecture, interiors, visualisation and execution under one roof.

Our Story

Founded on the idea that good buildings come from good conversation.

Studio ARQ began as a small atelier focused on private residences. Today, we work across residential, commercial, healthcare and hospitality sectors — but the founding sensibility hasn't changed: every project starts with a brief written together with the client, in plain language, and every drawing answers a specific question raised in that brief.

Our team brings together architects, interior designers, visualisation artists and site supervisors. We deliberately stay small so that the principal who pitches your project is the same one who sketches it, draws the joinery, and walks the site with the contractor.

What We Bring

Four disciplines, one design language.

A bespoke residence and a commercial fit-out look very different on paper, but they share the same underlying rigour at our studio — site analysis, climate strategy, material discipline, and a relentless focus on the joint.

Studio Principles

What we believe.

Four ideas we keep coming back to — in concept reviews, on site, and in every client conversation.

Listen first

We start with conversations — about how you live, work and entertain — before we ever pick up a pencil.

Draw everything

If it's going to be built, it gets drawn at full detail. No on-site improvisation, no contractor guesswork.

Honest materials

We prefer real timber to laminate, real stone to veneer, real plaster to texture paint — where the budget allows.

Stay small

One studio, one signature. The principal who hears your brief is the same one who walks the site.
