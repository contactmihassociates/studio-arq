The Studio

A small studio with a long brief.

studio arqs is an independent architectural practice founded by Abdul Azeem in Chennai. We design at every scale — from a single room to a multi-storey commercial — and we keep architecture, interiors, visualisation and execution under one roof.

Our Story

Founded on the idea that good buildings come from good conversation.

Full profile LinkedIn

studio arqs is led by Abdul Azeem. His career began in Chennai, took him to Jeddah for over five years at SAUDI ABV General Contractors, and brought him back to Chennai in 2019 to join studio arqs as Senior Architect — a role he led for five years before being promoted to Principal Architect in April 2024.

Today, we work across residential, commercial, healthcare and hospitality sectors — but the founding sensibility hasn't changed: every project starts with a brief written together with the client, in plain language, and every drawing answers a specific question raised in that brief.

We deliberately stay small so that the principal who pitches your project is the same one who sketches it, draws the joinery, and walks the site with the contractor.

Studio Principles

What we believe.

Four ideas we keep coming back to — in concept reviews, on site, and in every client conversation.

Listen first

We start with conversations — about how you live, work and entertain — before we ever pick up a pencil.

Draw everything

If it's going to be built, it gets drawn at full detail. No on-site improvisation, no contractor guesswork.

Honest materials

We prefer real timber to laminate, real stone to veneer, real plaster to texture paint — where the budget allows.

Stay small

One studio, one signature. The principal who hears your brief is the same one who walks the site.
