Meet

Abd Al Azeem

Founder & Principal Architect · Studio ARQ

An architect by training, an obsessive detailer by habit, and a Chennai resident by choice. Abd Al Azeem founded Studio ARQ to do something specific — design buildings the way a small studio used to, with one principal in charge from the first site visit to the last cushion.

Over the last few years his work has spanned private residences, mid-rise apartment blocks, healthcare interiors and hospitality plazas — all out of a small, focused studio. The thread connecting them is a stubborn insistence on craft: that the joint between two materials is a tiny ethical decision, and that a building isn't done until somebody can run their hand across a sill without flinching.

When he's not at the studio or on site, you'll most likely find him sketching at a café, photographing facades on the morning walk, or thinking out loud on the studio's Instagram — @studio_arqs.

The Journey

Studio ARQ, year by year.

A short timeline of how the studio has grown — and the kinds of projects that have shaped its sensibility.

2020

Studio ARQ is founded

Abd Al Azeem starts the studio in Chennai with a focus on bespoke private residences — designed end-to-end out of a small home office.

2022

First commercial commissions

The studio's first healthcare and hospitality interiors are designed — broadening the practice beyond residential.

2024

Move into mid-rise apartments

Bloom Apartments and a series of boutique apartment briefs put the studio on the map for residential developers in Chennai.

2025

40+ projects, six sectors

The studio crosses 40 delivered projects across residential, apartments, hospitality, healthcare, commercial and pure interior fit-outs.

2026

Hussain Haji ships

The Kattupakam residence — a corten-toned facade with a vertical garden screen — is shipped, and becomes the studio's most-discussed render set.
