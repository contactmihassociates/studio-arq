Meet

Abdul Azeem . S . A

Principal Architect · studio arq · Chennai

Architect with over 13 years of experience, a proven track record of leading design development, managing client relationships and delivering high-quality designs. Skilled in resolving complex design issues, coordinating with contractors, and ensuring compliance with regulations.

Abdul Azeem holds a Bachelor of Architecture in Architecture & Interior Design from MEASI Academy of Architecture (2006 – 2011) and is a registered architect with the Council of Architecture of India (Credential ID CA/2012/57306, issued Oct 2012). His career began in Chennai (Eta Star, KKR Architects), continued in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia (ALUMCO, then SAUDI ABV General Contractors as Architect for 5 yrs 7 mos), and returned to Chennai in 2019 when he joined studio arq as Senior Architect. He was promoted to Principal Architect in April 2024.

Proficient in AutoCAD, SketchUp, Twin Motion and Revit (Autodesk Revit Architecture 2019 certified), Abdul leads every brief at the studio personally — from site visit through joinery sheets to the final snag list. The studio publishes its work on Instagram at @studio_arqs.
