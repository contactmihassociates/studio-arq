Carpet ↔ Built-up ↔ Super area.

Three different ways to measure the same flat. Enter what your builder gave you in any one and see the other two — with realistic Chennai loading factors.

Defaults are typical for Chennai mid-rise residential: 15% for walls + 25% for common areas. Apartments with more amenities go higher.
